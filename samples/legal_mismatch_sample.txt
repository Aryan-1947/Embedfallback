This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and
between the Provider and the Client. Whereas Provider possesses certain expertise and Client
desires to retain such services, the parties agree as follows.

Section 1: Scope of Services. Provider shall render the services described in Exhibit A
attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference. Any modification to the scope of
services shall require a written amendment executed by both parties.

Section 2: Indemnification. Each party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other
party from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, and expenses, including
reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to a breach of this Agreement or the
indemnifying party's negligence or willful misconduct.

Section 3: Limitation of Liability. In no event shall either party's aggregate liability
under this Agreement exceed the total fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim.

Section 4: Governing Law and Dispute Resolution. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws
of the applicable jurisdiction without regard to conflict of law principles. Any dispute
arising hereunder shall first be subject to good-faith negotiation, and failing resolution,
shall be submitted to binding arbitration.
